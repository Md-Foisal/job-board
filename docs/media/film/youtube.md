## Description

Laravel job board with Livewire 4 and Filament 5, where every application has a timeline the candidate can follow. Live: https://hearbackjobs.com
Full stack Laravel 13 project by Md. Foisal: CV builder, applicant board, employer analytics, staff moderation. Code: https://github.com/Md-Foisal/job-board

On most job boards an application can just disappear. HearBack is a job board (job portal) built so that a company's silence shows:
- Every application has its own timeline, with each stage change and the final answer.
- Employers who answer the people who apply get a "Responsive employer" mark.
- Candidates can review a company's hiring process, and the company can answer in public.
- Staff read a new employer's job posts before the public sees them.
- A CV builder: seven templates, PDF and Word export, a CV aimed at one job.
- For employers: an applicant board by stage and analytics for each posting.

Everything in this film is the running app:
00:00 Opening
00:14 The candidate: search, match, apply
00:36 The employer: applicants, stages, an answer
01:01 Silence shows: the responsive mark and reviews
01:14 The CV builder
01:30 Analytics and moderation
01:45 The details and the build

Stack: Laravel 13, Livewire 4, Flux UI, Filament 5, Tailwind CSS 4, MySQL, Pest (over 1,590 tests).

About the demo: this is a portfolio project and the film uses seeded demo data. Every company and person in it is made up, and the pictures are drawn avatars. The live site runs with its AI features off, on free hosting.

Music: written for this film and synthesised in code. No third-party recordings.
